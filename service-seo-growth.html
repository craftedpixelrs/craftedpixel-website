01

Technical SEO

Crawl budget, indexation, canonicals, XML sitemaps, and structured data—foundation work that lets Google trust what you publish.

We audit like engineers: log analysis hints, render parity, and duplicate URL patterns that quietly split signals. Fixes land as tickets with severity, effort, and expected impact—not a 200-slide deck.

Core Web Vitals and mobile usability tie directly to rankings and CRO; we align with your dev backlog so perf wins don’t stall in “nice to have.”

Typical outputs

  • Prioritized technical backlog with owners
  • Robots, sitemap, and canonical recommendations
  • Schema plan (Article, Product, FAQ where valid)
  • Pre/post deploy checklist for releases

Best when

Traffic flatlined after a replatform, staging leaks to index, or GSC shows persistent coverage errors.

02

On-page & content SEO

Intent mapping, heading hierarchy, internal links, and editor-friendly briefs—so new pages rank without reinventing the wheel each time.

We group queries by intent, map them to URL patterns, and define template-level rules (titles, meta, body structure) your CMS can enforce.

When you lack volume, we focus on bottom-funnel and comparison content first—where conversions justify the investment.

Typical outputs

  • Keyword / intent map tied to templates
  • Content brief templates & exemplars
  • Internal link recommendations
  • Editor checklist for publish QA

Best when

Editorial teams ship fast but pages compete with each other, or thin templates dilute topical authority.

03

Migrations & redirects

Domain, subdomain, or CMS moves with 1:1 mapping, staged testing, and post-launch monitoring—so equity and backlinks transfer cleanly.

We inventory URLs, backlinks, and traffic drivers before cutover; redirect rules are tested in staging with sample old→new pairs.

After launch we watch GSC coverage, server logs, and ranked URL sets—rolling back or patching rules before losses harden.

Typical outputs

  • Redirect map (CSV) + edge-case rules
  • Pre-launch crawl diff report
  • Post-launch 7/30-day monitoring plan
  • Stakeholder comms template for URL changes

Best when

Rebrand, HTTPS cutover, headless move, or merger integration where organic is a board-level KPI.

04

Measurement & Search Console

GSC hygiene, query–page monitoring, and dashboards that connect rankings to landing engagement—not just green arrows in a slide.

We segment branded vs. non-branded, track striking distance terms, and flag anomalies (CTR cliffs, sudden impression drops) with likely causes.

Where useful we connect GA4 landing engagement and conversion paths so SEO prioritization respects revenue—not only clicks.

Typical outputs

  • GSC property cleanup & user access model
  • Monthly or weekly scorecard template
  • Alert thresholds for coverage & CWV
  • Annotation log for releases & migrations

Best when

Leadership wants an organic KPI you can defend, or marketing lost trust in last agency’s opaque reporting.

05

Growth experiments

Hypothesis-led title/meta tests, snippet tuning, and landing variants—small bets with readouts tied to CTR, CVR, or qualified leads.

We document a hypothesis, success metric, and minimum sample before changing live pages—avoiding endless tweak loops.

Experiments pair with analytics events so you learn even when the headline test is flat.

Typical outputs

  • Experiment backlog with ICE scoring
  • Before/after SERP capture where applicable
  • Template for scaling winners across URLs
  • Guardrails so tests don’t cannibalize brand

Best when

You have traffic but weak CTR, or landing pages match queries poorly once users arrive.
