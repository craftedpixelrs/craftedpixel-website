Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you browse getcraftedpixel.com, email us, or start a project conversation.

Privacy · Terms · Cookies

Last updated: April 5, 2026 · Controller: CraftedPixel — New York, NY, USA & Novi Sad, Serbia · getcraftedpixel.com

Who we are

CraftedPixel is a digital design and engineering studio. This policy describes how we process personal information in connection with our public website and routine studio communications. For client engagements, separate agreements (statements of work, NDAs, or data processing terms) may apply to project data.

What we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may process:

  • Contact details you send us (for example name, email address, company, phone number, and the content of your message) when you use Contact, email, or similar channels. Structuring your first note with our public kickoff brief checklist is optional but helps us reply with fewer back-and-forth questions.
  • Careers information if you apply for a role (résumé, portfolio links, and correspondence).
  • Technical data that browsers and servers typically exchange automatically, such as IP address, general location derived from IP, device type, browser type, referring URL, and timestamps—often recorded in standard server or security logs.

How we use information

We use personal information to respond to inquiries, evaluate fit for projects or roles, operate and secure our website, comply with law, and improve clarity of our content. We do not sell your personal information.

Legal bases (where applicable)

If you are in the European Economic Area or UK, we rely on legitimate interests (responding to business inquiries, site security, and studio operations) and, where needed, contract or consent—for example if you subscribe to a mailing list we may add in the future.

Retention

We keep messages and project-related correspondence for as long as needed to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. Technical logs are kept for a limited period consistent with security and troubleshooting needs.

Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us run the business (for example email, hosting, or analytics configured in a privacy-respecting way), subject to confidentiality and purpose limitations. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold, subject to legal exceptions. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA (including knowing what we collect, requesting deletion, and opting out of sale—note we do not sell personal information). To exercise rights, email office@getcraftedpixel.com.

International transfers

We are based in the United States. If you contact us from another country, your information may be processed in the US or other locations where we or our providers operate.

Children

Our site and services are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes may be highlighted on the site or by email where appropriate.

Contact

Questions about privacy: office@getcraftedpixel.com · CraftedPixel (New York, NY & Novi Sad, Serbia). Serbian market: craftedpixel.rs · founder: marko.banovic@craftedpixel.rs.

Cookie-specific details: see our Cookie Policy.
