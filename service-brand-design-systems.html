How we plug in

We connect narrative, visual language, and implementation constraints early—so your first landing page and your fiftieth product screen share tokens, type scales, and motion rules that engineers can adopt.

  • Identity sprint — mark, color, type, and voice starters for launch deadlines.
  • System program — tokens, components, and docs across web + product.

01

Logo & visual identity

Marks, color systems, typography, and usage rules tested on real touchpoints—favicons, app icons, slide decks, and social—not only a hero mockup.

We explore positioning tension first: who you are for, who you are not for, and the few attributes you want recalled. Visual directions are scored against accessibility, motion, and production constraints.

Deliverables include master artwork, safe zones, minimum sizes, and monochrome / reversed treatments so partners don’t improvise under pressure.

Typical outputs

  • Logo suite (SVG, PNG, app icon exports)
  • Color ramps with contrast pairs (WCAG-aware)
  • Primary & secondary type stacks with fallbacks
  • Competitive differentiation notes

Best when

Funding milestone, rebrand after M&A, or your Series A story outgrew the bootstrap mark.

02

Design systems

Semantic tokens, spacing scales, elevation, and naming conventions that map cleanly to CSS variables or platform themes.

We define primitives then semantic aliases (e.g., surface.default, text.muted) so redesigns don’t require renaming every component.

Documentation covers contribution, versioning, and deprecation—so the system survives your next hire spike.

Typical outputs

  • Token table (JSON / Style Dictionary–ready)
  • Theming notes (light/dark, brand accents)
  • Component taxonomy & status model
  • Governance outline for proposals & reviews

Best when

Product and marketing diverged; every screen invents new grays and radii.

03

Component libraries

Figma kits and coded counterparts with props, variants, and states that match how engineers actually compose screens.

We align on atomic vs. composite boundaries, slot patterns for marketing vs. product, and how content editors will stress each component.

When Storybook or similar is in play, we mirror naming and default props so design QA has a single checklist.

Typical outputs

  • Figma library with variants & docs
  • Starter coded components (scope-dependent)
  • Interaction & motion specs for key patterns
  • QA checklist for new contributions

Best when

Engineers rebuild the same buttons and cards because the library never shipped—or drifted from Figma.

04

Brand guidelines & templates

Slide, social, and email patterns with do/don’t examples—short enough that teams open them weekly.

We prioritize scenarios your team repeats: pitch decks, changelog posts, hiring banners, and partner co-mark. Each gets a template, not a paragraph of adjectives.

Optional Notion or microsite format so search and updates beat the frozen PDF problem.

Typical outputs

  • Guidelines site or PDF (your workflow)
  • Template files (Figma / Slides / email HTML as scoped)
  • Voice & tone examples with before/after
  • Asset download hub structure

Best when

Distributed teams freestyle brand on every campaign; legal or partners ask for official rules.

What a typical two weeks looks like

Identity and system work broken into reviewable milestones.

Week 1

  • Stakeholder interviews & competitive scan
  • Creative directions (mood + rationale)
  • Shortlist direction with usage tests

Week 2

  • Refine master assets & color/type ramps
  • Seed token set + first 5 components
  • Guidelines outline for templates

What we need from you

  • Decision-makers available for consolidated feedback
  • Any existing brand assets or legal trademark constraints
  • Target surfaces (web, iOS, slide, etc.)
  • Engineering contact for token import path
