Overview

Keser Inženjering operates across civil engineering, construction delivery, and related technical services. The public site needs to communicate capability to procurement teams and partners—without sounding like a generic template.

We’re aligning service pages, proof points, and contact paths with how bids and referrals actually happen, while standing up design & analytics (D&A): event naming, conversion goals, and dashboards leadership can trust. Detailed metrics and final screenshots will ship with the completed case study.

The constraint

Industrial and infrastructure buyers don’t impulse-click “contact us.” They scan credentials, past work categories, and clarity of scope—often on mobile between site visits.

We’re prioritizing credible project framing (sectors, geographies, engagement types) and a measurement layer that ties campaigns and referrals to real inquiries—not vanity sessions.

We’re tightening how we show what we actually build—and making sure marketing can prove which channels bring qualified RFQs.

— Client stakeholder · Keser Inženjering (paraphrased; pending final approval)

Approach

Iterating in design → staging → analytics QA loops: ship readable service architecture first, then layer D&A (events, goals, Looker Studio / GA4 views) so the team can read funnels within a week of launch.

  • IA & wireframes for services, projects, and contact flows
  • UI system tied to brand neutrals + engineering photography direction
  • D&A: measurement plan, GA4/GTM baseline, quarterly review cadence
  • Core Web Vitals budget checks on hero and project templates

Design system in production

Lightweight component library for long-form case pages, equipment galleries, and certificate / partner rows—so new projects don’t break layout rhythm.

Tokens cover typography scale, spacing, and CTA hierarchy so non-designers can publish in the CMS without drifting off-brand.

Results

In progress. We’re validating inquiry quality, organic visibility for priority service terms, and event completeness in GA4. Final before/after metrics and charts will publish here once the engagement milestone is signed off.

Early internal targets: faster time-to-first-qualified-lead from key landing pages and fewer bounced mobile sessions on project detail templates.
