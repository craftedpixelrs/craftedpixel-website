Who we are

CraftedPixel is a small, senior-heavy team. We take on a limited number of engagements so principals stay in the work—discovery, design, build, and launch—not just the sales deck. Most of our relationships are repeat or referral because we optimize for trust and delivery, not headcount.

Whether you need a focused sprint or a partner through GA, we anchor on outcomes: something your users can touch, your stakeholders can align on, and your team can maintain.

Day to day, our roadmaps skew toward B2B SaaS, DTC & e-commerce, and fintech—the same lanes as our published case studies—with a clear product or marketing owner in the thread.

  • Based — New York, NY & Novi Sad, Serbia; collaborators and clients worldwide.
  • Stack-agnostic — we meet you in React, Next.js, WordPress, or your existing toolchain.
  • Honest fit — if we are not the right team, we will say so and point you elsewhere. See when we’re usually not a fit.

Our goals

What we optimize for on every engagement.

01

Outcomes over output

Shipping is table stakes. We care whether the release moves the metrics you care about—conversion, activation, time-to-task—and we align scope so design and engineering stay tied to those signals.

02

Respect for your team

We plug into your tools, speak plainly with engineering, and document decisions so you are not dependent on us forever. The goal is a handoff you can own.

03

Calm, predictable delivery

Fixed milestones, visible increments, and one accountable thread of communication. No surprise layers, no black-box agency theater.

Quality bar

Accessibility, performance, and data hygiene—by default.

We don’t treat inclusive UX or fast loads as a separate line item. Here’s what that means in practice on our own site and in client work.

Accessibility

Keyboard paths, focus order, labels, and contrast are part of how we design—not a late QA pass. We target WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline for marketing sites and product work unless a brief explicitly scopes otherwise.

Performance

We optimize critical rendering, image delivery, and bundle discipline so experiences stay fast on real devices and networks. For us, performance is part of brand and conversion—not an abstract Lighthouse trophy.

Privacy & cookies

We keep tracking on our marketing site minimal and explain what we collect. Read the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy; engagement data is governed by your SOW and DP terms when we work together.

How we work

The operating system behind our projects.

Same principles as on the homepage—because this is how we actually run engagements, not how we wish we did.

Senior-led, always

The people in the conversation are the people on the timeline. No bait-and-switch to juniors after kickoff.

Fixed milestones

Two-week slices with something reviewable each cycle—working software, prototypes, or specs your team can implement.

Direct communication

Shared channel, weekly rhythm, and a single point of contact. Feedback is batched so budgets and morale stay intact.

Vision

Digital products that feel inevitable—not accidental.

We believe the best sites and apps disappear into the task: fast, accessible, and honest about what they ask of the user. Our vision is to help teams get there without hiring a department—by pairing strategy, craft, and implementation in one accountable loop.

  • Accessibility — inclusive defaults, not a late QA checkbox.
  • Performance — speed and clarity as brand signals.
  • Measurement — define success before we polish pixels.
