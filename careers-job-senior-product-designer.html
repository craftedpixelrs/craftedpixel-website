Evergreen hiring

CraftedPixel is growing quickly, and we plan to expand our team in this area in the near future. Even when we do not yet have a formally approved opening for every role profile listed here, we still want to meet exceptional people we can build the future with.

Please apply and send your résumé or portfolio so you are among the first we contact when this position is officially opened. We expect that to happen in the coming months, depending on client demand and our internal planning timeline.

About the role

You lead discovery and delivery for client engagements and occasional internal tools—turning ambiguous briefs into shippable UX, documented in Figma and validated next to engineering. You are as comfortable in dense B2B workflows as you are polishing marketing surfaces when the same brand has to show up in both places.

You will work directly with principals and engineers—no handoff-only layer. Expect async-first communication with focused live sessions for reviews and workshops.

Responsibilities

  • Facilitate structured discovery with clients—goals, constraints, success metrics, and honest tradeoffs.
  • Produce flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity UI; maintain libraries and handoff specs engineering can build from.
  • Run or support usability sessions; synthesize findings into prioritized changes.
  • Partner with engineering on accessibility, performance, and realistic edge states (empty, loading, error).
  • Contribute to design QA before launch; document patterns so teams can extend the system after handoff.

Requirements

  • Strong portfolio showing end-to-end product work—not only marketing sites.
  • Expert-level Figma; comfortable with variants, auto-layout, and component architecture.
  • Working knowledge of WCAG 2.2 AA patterns and how they affect layout and components.
  • Experience collaborating in async-first teams (written updates, Loom, shared boards).
  • Authorization to work in the United States without employer sponsorship.

Nice to have

  • Design-system documentation or token workflows (e.g. Style Dictionary, Figma variables).
  • Familiarity with React or HTML/CSS enough to speak credibly with frontend engineers.
  • Experience in hiring, HR tech, or other multi-role enterprise products.

Compensation & benefits

Part-time compensation: Pro-rated from a full-time equivalent of USD $140,000–$170,000 base (roughly $70–$85/hr for engaged hours, depending on experience), or an agreed monthly retainer—your offer letter will spell out the exact structure.

  • Health, dental, and vision available for US W-2 part-time roles meeting eligibility thresholds; otherwise cash stipend toward coverage.
  • Pro-rated PTO and holidays aligned to your FTE fraction.
  • Remote home office / coworking stipend (pro-rated).
  • Professional development budget for books, courses, and conferences.

Working conditions & policies

  • Hours: Part-time (typically 24–30 hours / week); core collaboration aligned with US Pacific, flexible async outside that window.
  • Equipment: Company laptop and tools; details confirmed at offer.
  • Travel: Rare; occasional client workshops when mutually agreed.
  • Background check: May be required after a verbal offer, before start date.
  • Confidentiality: You may see client roadmaps; standard NDA / MSA terms apply.

CraftedPixel is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, color, national origin, gender, sexual orientation, age, marital status, veteran status, or disability status.

Hiring process

  1. Application review (portfolio + short answers or cover note).
  2. 30-minute intro with a design lead.
  3. Take-home or paid design exercise—your choice of policy; we recommend paid for work over ~2 hours.
  4. Final round: culture and collaboration with principals.
  5. Reference checks and written offer.

Apply

Send an email with portfolio link, PDF or deck (under 8 MB), and a short note on one product you shipped—your role, constraints, and outcome. Use the subject line we pre-fill so triage stays fast.
