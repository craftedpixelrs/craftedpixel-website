Kickoff brief checklist

Align your team before the first workshop or SOW—goals, links, stakeholders, and success criteria in one place. Copy into your doc, print, or save as PDF; no form required.

How we work · Cadence & first round · Scope wizard · Contact

Updated: April 5, 2026 · Use: internal prep or attach to your first email. See Privacy for how we handle messages.

Goals & success

What does “done” look like for this phase?

  • One-sentence goal for the next 4–8 weeks (not the five-year vision).
  • 2–3 measurable signals (conversion, activation, time-to-task, support volume—whatever fits).
  • Known risks or past failures we should not repeat.
  • What is explicitly out of scope for this slice.

Audience & context

Who uses this, and what do they need to accomplish?

  • Primary user segments (roles, regions, devices, accessibility needs).
  • Top jobs-to-be-done or critical flows (onboarding, checkout, settings, etc.).
  • Competitors or references you like—and what specifically you like.
  • Content that must stay, migrate, or retire.

Brand & creative inputs

Everything we should treat as source of truth.

  • Links to Figma, brand guidelines, or component libraries.
  • Logo, color, type, and motion rules (or “none yet—help us define”).
  • Photography / illustration direction and assets we can use.
  • Words to avoid and tone notes (plain, playful, enterprise, etc.).

Tech, access & analytics

So we do not burn a week chasing logins.

  • Hosting, repo, CMS, and who can grant access.
  • Staging vs production URLs and deployment expectations.
  • Analytics or experimentation tools in use (or “none—recommend”).
  • Compliance constraints (cookie consent, HIPAA, SOC2, etc.) if any.

Stakeholders & decisions

How feedback actually lands.

  • Named owner for day-to-day decisions and who has final sign-off.
  • Review cadence that fits your team (async + one weekly live slot, etc.).
  • Legal, procurement, or brand reviewers who must see work before launch.
  • Slack / email / project tool we should use.

Timeline & constraints

Calendar truth, not wishful thinking.

  • Hard dates (launch, event, board) vs flexible windows.
  • Budget guardrails or procurement steps we should plan for.
  • Dependencies on other vendors or internal teams.
  • Time zones and meeting windows that work for approvers.

This checklist is guidance, not a contract. For cadence targets and revision policy on real engagements, see How we work → Cadence & first round. Questions? office@getcraftedpixel.com.
