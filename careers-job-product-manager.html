Evergreen hiring

CraftedPixel is growing quickly, and we plan to expand our team in this area in the near future. Even when we do not yet have a formally approved opening for every role profile listed here, we still want to meet exceptional people we can build the future with.

Please apply and send your résumé or portfolio so you are among the first we contact when this position is officially opened. We expect that to happen in the coming months, depending on client demand and our internal planning timeline.

About the role

You are the operational spine for multi-quarter engagements—turning statements of work into milestones, running weekly client rituals, and making sure scope, risk, and quality stay visible.

You will work in English with US-based leads and in Serbian with the local team; comfort code-switching and writing crisp async updates is essential.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain roadmaps, release plans, and RAID logs; surface tradeoffs early.
  • Facilitate sprint planning, demos, and retros with design and engineering pods.
  • Translate client feedback into prioritized tickets with acceptance criteria.
  • Coordinate UAT, launch checklists, and handover documentation.
  • Track utilization and flag resourcing risks to principals.

Requirements

  • Proven experience shipping software or large web projects with external clients.
  • Strong English (C1+) for calls and writing; fluent Serbian for local collaboration.
  • Comfort with Jira, Linear, or equivalent; can build a board that executives actually read.
  • Understanding of design and engineering workflows—you do not need to code, but you cannot fear tickets.
  • Eligible to work full-time in Serbia from our Novi Sad location.

Nice to have

  • Experience with agency retainers or fixed-bid + change-order models.
  • Familiarity with Figma comments and dev handoff basics.

Compensation & benefits

Gross monthly range: competitive for senior PM level in Vojvodina, discussed individually. Includes statutory benefits, paid annual leave, and private health per local package.

  • Hybrid schedule with core in-studio days.
  • Hardware and tools provided.
  • English coaching budget if you want to polish presentation skills.

Working conditions & policies

  • Hours: Overlap with US West Coast mornings several days per week; remainder flexible.
  • Travel: Occasional EU or US travel for key workshops (passport ready).
  • Confidentiality: Client NDAs; no side work that conflicts with active accounts.

CraftedPixel is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, color, national origin, gender, sexual orientation, age, marital status, veteran status, or disability status.

Hiring process

  1. CV + short note on a complex delivery you untangled.
  2. 45-minute interview with operations lead (mix of EN/SR).
  3. Scenario workshop: prioritize a fake backlog under pressure.
  4. Reference check and offer.

Apply

Send an email with CV (PDF), LinkedIn if you use it, and two sentences on the messiest delivery you have untangled. Use the subject line we pre-fill.
